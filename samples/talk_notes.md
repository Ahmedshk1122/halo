# Onboarding update — talk notes

1. Thanks for coming. Quick update on onboarding this quarter.
2. Headline: activation went from 31 to 44 percent over the quarter. Biggest jump since launch.
3. The problem: drop-off is concentrated at workspace setup. Almost everyone verifies their email, then many stall there.
4. Story: the design studio founder who saw an empty workspace and closed the tab.
5. Proposal, three moves:
   - templates instead of a blank page
   - imports from the tools people already use
   - invites later rather than first
6. Picture it: you open the app for the first time and see ready-made templates and one clear button to start from scratch.
7. Rollout: templates in November, imports in December, invites moved later in January.
8. Back to the funnel: the workspace step is what all three moves target.
9. The ask: back the plan, help us ship templates in November.
