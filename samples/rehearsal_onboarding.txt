## greeting
Hi everyone, thanks for joining this morning.
It's good to see so many of you here, and I know it's a busy week.
[pause 1]

## headline
Let's start with the headline.
Over the quarter, activation went from 31 to 44%.
That's the biggest jump we've had since launch, and it held up week after week.
[pause 1]

## drop-off
But we are still losing a lot of people on the way in.
When you look at where they leave, the drop-off is concentrated at one step, setting up a workspace.
Almost everyone verifies their email, and then a big share of them stall right there.
[pause 1]

## anecdote
I talked to a customer last week, she runs a small design studio.
She told me she opened the app, saw an empty workspace, and just closed the tab.
She only came back because a colleague invited her a month later.
That story really stuck with me.
[pause 1]

## proposal
So here's what we want to do about it, and it comes down to three moves.
First, templates instead of a blank page.
Second, imports from the tools people already use.
And third, invites later rather than first.
[pause 1]

## picture
Picture opening the app for the first time, and you see this.
A set of ready-made templates for the work you already do, and one clear button to start from scratch.
[pause 1]

## rollout
Here's how we'd roll it out.
Templates ship in November.
Imports follow in December.
And in January we move invites later in the flow.
[pause 1]

## recall
Halo, back to the funnel.
That workspace step is exactly what all three moves are aimed at.
[pause 1]

## close
So here's my ask.
Back the plan today, and help us ship templates in November.
Thank you.
